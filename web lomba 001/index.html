About us

A small studio that treats paint like engineering.

AutoForge started as a weekend project: a way to see a car in your colors before anyone touches a spray gun. Today it is a design studio and a 3D tool. Customers play with the same models our designers use.

Every car on this site is built from code, panel by panel, so each part has its own material. That is why you can repaint a hood without touching the doors.

Panel-level control

Body, hood, roof, doors, bumpers, trim, rims, calipers and lights each carry their own color and finish.

Inside and out

Switch to the cabin cutaway to set seats, dashboard, carpet, headliner and ambient light.

Honest preview

Real reflections, shadows and five paint finishes, so the screen looks close to the garage.

Goals and aim

Where we are heading.

  1. Anyone should be able to shape a car without learning CAD. Every control here is one click, and every change shows at once.

  2. Test a color on screen first. Fewer test panels and repaints means less solvent and less scrap.

  3. Coupes, vans and electric hatchbacks are next, each with the same panel-level control.

  4. Send a saved build straight to a partner workshop with a parts list and a paint code.
